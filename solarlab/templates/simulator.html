Experiment Angle θ Weather Output (W)

The power comes from Lambert's cosine law, P = Pmax × w × I × (1 − s) × cos(θ), for a 100 W panel. Here w is the weather factor (1.0 sunny, 0.6 partly cloudy, 0.25 cloudy), I is the sunlight intensity from 0 to 1 and s is the fraction of the panel in shade (0 unless you include shade). The weather factors are assumed values for the demonstration, not measurements. The colour styles are only inspired by popular websites and are not connected to them.
